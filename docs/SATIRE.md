# About the satire

**BTA 7 Almosh City is a comedy.** It began as an inside joke among friends who grew up around
Beer Sheva and was opened to everyone in November 2026. This page says what the joke is, and
what it isn't, so that people who find the repository without that background know what they
are looking at.

## What it does

The game turns the city's crowds into fictional crime gangs and exaggerates each one the way
locals joke about each other: the traders who will sell you anything, even in the middle of a
chase; the tuned-car crowd who never start a fight and never let one go; the chair crowd whose
weapon is the white plastic chair they were sitting on; the students who vanish every Thursday;
the grandmothers who outrank all of them; and the parking inspectors, who are everybody's enemy.

The rule is that **everyone gets it equally**. No group is the villain and no group is spared.

## What it is not

- **Not a portrait of real people.** Every gang, name and character is invented. Real communities
  in Beer Sheva are far more varied than any joke about them.
- **Not a political statement.** It takes no side in anything and is not about any real conflict.
- **Not hateful.** There are no slurs, and nothing in the game treats any group as less than
  human. Content that does is not accepted (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md#ground-rules-for-content)).

## The name

"BTA 7" is a parody title in the tradition of genre jokes. The project has no connection to any
commercial game, publisher or brand. It uses no names, logos, fonts, music, artwork, models or
other assets from one, and contributions that do will be removed.

## If something crosses the line

Humour is personal, and a line written for friends can read differently to strangers. If you
think something in the game is hateful rather than funny, open an issue and say which line and
why. It will be read with an open mind.

## על הסאטירה

**BTA 7 אלמוש סיטי הוא קומדיה.** הוא התחיל כבדיחה פרטית של חברים שגדלו ליד באר שבע, ונפתח לכולם
בנובמבר 2026. המשחק הופך את הקבוצות בעיר לכנופיות פשע בדיוניות ומגזים כל אחת כמו שמקומיים צוחקים
אחד על השני. הכלל: **כולם חוטפים באותה מידה.** אין קבוצה שהיא הרעה ואין קבוצה שיוצאת נקייה.

כל הכנופיות, השמות והדמויות בדיוניים, ואין כאן דיוקן של אנשים או קהילות אמיתיים. אין במשחק קללות
גזעניות ואין הצהרה פוליטית. "BTA 7" הוא שם פרודי; לפרויקט אין קשר לשום משחק, מפרסם או מותג
מסחרי, ואין בו שום נכס שלהם.

חושבים שמשהו עבר את הגבול? פתחו issue, ציינו איזו שורה ולמה.
