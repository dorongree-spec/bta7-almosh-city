# Avraham Avinu street: what Doron's Street View walk shows

Doron recorded a minute of Google Street View along the street, from
no. 1 at the bottom up to no. 26 (pictures of November 2018, and of
October 2017 at the David HaMelech corner). These are notes from watching
it. The recording itself is not in the repository and nothing from it is
used as a texture; it was looked at, and the street was built from what it
shows.

Positions are in the game's metres. The street runs from about
(613, -2387) at no. 1, north-west and then due north, to the corner of
David HaMelech at about (532, -2622), and on past the Gilat centre.
The game opens at no. 15, about (536, -2554).

## Bottom of the street, no. 1 to 3

- West side: a small **playground** under a sand-coloured shade sail,
  behind a **green railing** of uprights with a top rail; on the pavement
  edge, green hoop barriers.
- East side, at no. 3: a **kiosk** selling nuts and seeds, a low white box
  with a red-and-white striped band, a counter under an awning, a large
  black sign with pink letters and a white flower on the roof, and racks
  outside. A red-roofed **bus shelter** stands in front of it and a full
  **car park** behind it, then a long four-storey block.
- A white nine-storey slab tower shows over the roofs to the north-east.
- Trees: big pines, a poinciana, ficus. They overhang the road.

## No. 9 to 10

- The road curves. A clipped **hedge** follows the inside of the bend.
- Blocks of three and four storeys stand **above the street on a bank**,
  behind **walls of rough stone** laid crazy-paving fashion, with steps up
  to each entrance and bushes hanging over the wall.
- Two kinds of block side by side:
  - **ochre** (brown-yellow) plaster, darker and dirtier than the game's;
  - **white** with a **pink-red band** along every floor slab.
- On both: an air conditioner under nearly every window, white roll
  shutters, window boxes with flowers, and **panels of pierced concrete
  blocks** beside the windows and across the old balconies.
- Wooden poles with bundles of **overhead wires** crossing the street.
- Cars parked nose to tail on both sides, mostly small and white or
  silver; one bright pink, one yellow.
- Pavements of red-pink interlocking pavers; kerbs painted red and white.

## The David HaMelech corner

- A **steel lattice pylon** with a transformer stands at the corner, on a
  traffic island, with wires going off four ways.
- Traffic lights on black poles, a bus-route sign, 30 km/h signs.
- The **Gilat centre** front: a single-storey row of shops under one long
  white awning, a bakery in the middle, tables outside.
- Behind it a cream **tower of about fourteen storeys** with a regular
  grid of windows (the map has it, 43 m).
- Across the road another ochre four-storey block behind a stone wall.

## No. 22 to 26

- West side: more single-storey shops with rounded awnings and a covered
  bus stop; people waiting.
- The street opens out: a big **car park**, low houses with red roofs,
  and three white towers on the skyline to the north.

## What this asks of the game

Done in the cloud: stone walls along the whole street, the kiosk, the
playground and the pylon (`scripts/landmarks.gd`, `STREET_SET`).
For the PC, by eye: the ochre and the white-with-pink-band block fronts,
pierced-block panels beside the windows, more and bigger trees, the hedge
on the bend.
