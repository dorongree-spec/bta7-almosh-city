# Ha-Pina Ha-Hama: what Doron's Street View walk shows

Doron recorded a minute and a half of Google Street View from Derekh
Hebron to the bakery on the corner of KKL street. These are notes from
watching it. The recording itself is not in the repository and nothing
from it is used as a texture; it was looked at, and the corner was built
from what it shows.

Positions are in the game's metres. The map (OpenStreetMap) has the
bakery at about (427, 331), where KKL street, Derekh Hebron, Ha'al
street and Imber boulevard meet. It is **not** at the map's origin, which
is the other end of KKL street; an earlier version of the first mission
sent the player there by mistake.

## Derekh Hebron, walking towards the corner

- A wide junction with traffic lights, a zebra crossing and a big car park.
- The central bus station side: green and white buses, palms, a railing
  along a planted median with rocks.
- A two-storey row of shops, a car dealership, and a yellow **petrol
  station** under a big canopy (the map has four fuel points at about
  (520 to 565, 270 to 340)).

## The junction

- Traffic lights, flags on the lamp posts, a traffic island paved with
  white cobbles.

## The bakery row

- A **single-storey row of old sandstone**, Ottoman in style, with
  **arched doorways** and painted metal doors, blue and pink.
- Tiled lean-to roofs over the pavement, awnings, satellite dishes.
- The corner unit has a **wide service window**; people queue on the
  pavement.
- A large **red-and-white sign on the roof** with the name. It shares its
  frame with a soft-drink advert, which the game leaves out.
- A big **poinciana** at the end of the row.

## Up KKL street

- Single-storey shops, **black-framed seating enclosures** with clear
  plastic sheets, pavers with cars parked on them.

## What this asks of the game

Done in the cloud (`scripts/landmarks.gd`, `_pina_hama`): the stone row
with arches and doors, the lean-to, the service window and striped
awning, the roof sign with our own lettering, roof clutter, the
poinciana and one seating enclosure. The two plain outlines the map has
on that ground are left out (`CLEARED`). The first mission's queue spot
is the pavement in front of the service window.

Not done: the petrol station canopy, the cobbled island, flags on the
lamp posts, the bus station side. Sizes are by eye, not surveyed.
